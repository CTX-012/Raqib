### 4.5 NEW r3 finding — GPU-unavailable INFO log fires every tick

Captured during the V2 launch: in 8 minutes of `--log-level info`
operation, the line

```
INFO No NVIDIA GPU detected. VRAM, GPU power, and per-device
temperature metrics will be empty; CPU, RAM, lifecycle, governor,
and regression detection still work. nvml_detail=…
```

appeared **445 times** — once per tick. The full 60-minute log
weighed 1.81 MB; ≈ 1.5 MB of that is this single repeated line. At
the default 1 Hz tick rate this is ≈ 1.6 MB/h of duplicate INFO
spam, ≈ 38 MB/day on a no-GPU host.

This is the inverse of Tester 2 V3 finding 3 (S.0.7): they reported
the line was DEBUG-only at info level (invisible to operators); the
fix in [D-2] / `src/platform/gpu_nvidia.rs` made it INFO-level but
on every NVML re-init attempt rather than one-shot. Suggested fix:
move the `tracing::info!` to a `OnceLock<()>` guarded path (or
emit on first failure + on transition only) so it appears once at
startup.

Severity: **S3 visibility / log-volume**, not S1 — the data is
correct; it's just over-emitted.
