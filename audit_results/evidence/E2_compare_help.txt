Side-by-side baseline comparison across one or more models (latest.md Tier 3.7)

Usage: edge_monitor compare [OPTIONS] <MODELS>...

Arguments:
  <MODELS>...  Models to compare. At least one. Use `edge_monitor history` (no args) to list known models

Options:
      --runs <RUNS>  Number of recent runs per model to fold into the baseline [default: 10]
      --json         Emit JSON instead of a human-readable table
  -h, --help         Print help
