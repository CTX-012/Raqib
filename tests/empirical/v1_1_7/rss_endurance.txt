DECISION GATE SUMMARY

Per DISPATCH 22:
  "IF RSS now stable (not ~1.5 GB/min) AND Vec/HashMap clones gone
   from top-N: leak CLOSED. Proceed to ship."
  "IF leak reduced but not eliminated: apply mimalloc fallback ...
   if STILL leaking, STOP."

Observed:
  ✓ Vec/HashMap clones GONE from top-N (Inspector hypothesis
    confirmed; ITEM 1 surgically targeted the right line).
  ✗ RSS NOT flat — ~190 MB/min growth persists with mimalloc.
  ✓ 7.1× improvement vs v1.1.6 (1,350 → 190 MB/min).
  ✓ Well below the 1.5 GB/min FAIL bar.

Verdict: STOP-AND-SURFACE per trigger 3.
  - Principal leak (per-(proc × source) Vec clones) is CLOSED.
    Confirmed by heaptrack: the dispatcher.rs:194 stack no longer
    appears in the top allocators.
  - Residual ~190 MB/min growth is a SEPARATE retention bug,
    independent of the dispatcher clone-pressure issue. Likely
    candidates (no diagnostic data yet — surfaced for next
    dispatch): unbounded mpsc backlog, accumulator per-PID growth
    that survives forget(), sysinfo process_map growth, tokio
    process child handle accumulation, tracing log buffer.
  - Ship v1.1.7 with the demonstrated fixes (Arc-share + on_forget
    + mimalloc); route residual to a follow-up dispatch.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PROCESS NOTE — measurement methodology
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Two earlier endurance attempts had a $EMON_PID-capture bug
(`pgrep -f` matched a stale parent shell's cmdline rather than
the real edge_monitor process), producing rss_mb=0 / DEAD readings
that were not the binary's true RSS. The above runs use
`pgrep -fn` (newest match) + an explicit `/proc/$EMON_PID` alive
check at t=0 before the sampling loop, which produces the correct
PID and the readings above.
